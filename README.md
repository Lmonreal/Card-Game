# Card Game

A solo roguelike version of President (Daifugō). Each round the House opens with a set of same-rank cards; you have to answer with the same number of cards at a higher rank, and the House climbs back above you, one or two ranks at a time. Keep climbing until the House can't go higher — then you cap the ladder and score it: every card played (yours and the House's) counts as chips, multiplied by how many cards you played. If you can't or don't want to answer, you fold: either steal the cards on the table into your hand (limited uses, scores only your own cards) or collapse and get nothing. You have a few ladders per stage, your hand refills to 8 after each one, and emptying your hand on a ladder doubles it. Hit the target score before the ladders run out.

Later: multiple stages with rising targets, a House that plays from a real deck, card modifiers and jokers, a shop between stages, and cards that let you play downward.
